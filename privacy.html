Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 15, 2026

At Stratmont Investments, we take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what information we collect, why we need it, and how we keep it strictly confidential.

1. Information We Collect

To provide you with secure investment services, we collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Identification: Your name, email address, date of birth, and physical address provided during account creation.
  • Verification Data: Government issued ID documents and proof of address required to meet global KYC (Know Your Customer) standards.
  • Financial Information: Transaction histories, wallet addresses, and deposit/withdrawal records.
  • Technical Data: IP addresses, browser types, and device information to help us prevent fraud and secure your account.

2. How We Use Your Information

We don't collect data just for the sake of it. We use your information purely to:

  • Process your deposits, investments, and withdrawals securely.
  • Comply with international Anti Money Laundering (AML) laws.
  • Communicate important account updates, security alerts, and platform changes.
  • Improve our platform's user experience and technical performance.

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell your personal data to advertisers. Ever.

We only share your information with trusted third party partners when absolutely necessary to run our services. This includes:

  • Identity verification services (to check your ID documents).
  • Regulatory authorities or law enforcement, but only if we are legally compelled by a valid court order.

4. How We Protect Your Data

Your data is locked down. We use AES 256 encryption for all sensitive information stored on our servers. All web traffic between your browser and our platform is protected by TLS encryption. We also mandate Two Factor Authentication (2FA) to ensure that even if someone gets your password, they cannot access your account.

5. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (like the EU under GDPR), you have the right to request a copy of the data we hold on you, ask us to correct inaccuracies, or request that we delete your account and personal data entirely (subject to legal data retention requirements).

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about how your data is handled, please reach out to our privacy team at:
