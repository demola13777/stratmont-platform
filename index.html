Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to your most important considerations.

How does Stratmont mitigate risk in cryptocurrency?

We utilize delta neutral strategies, rigorous cold storage protocols, and limit our exposure solely to highly liquid, top tier digital assets. Our algorithmic models employ automated stop loss mechanisms to cap drawdowns.

What is the lock up period for investments?

Lock up periods vary by asset class. Cryptocurrency funds have a 90 day lock up, while Real Estate and Oil & Gas typically require a 12 to 24 month commitment due to the illiquid nature of the underlying physical assets.

How are dividends and profits distributed?

Depending on your chosen tier, profits are calculated and distributed at maturity of the 3-month cycle. Clients can choose to automatically reinvest dividends for compound growth or withdraw them directly to their linked bank accounts or crypto wallets.

Is Stratmont open to international investors?

Yes, we operate globally and accept clients from most jurisdictions, subject to successful completion of our strict KYC (Know Your Customer) and AML (Anti Money Laundering) verification processes.
