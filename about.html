Our Story

Bridging the Gap

Back in 2018, we noticed a frustrating double standard in the financial world. Institutional investors hedge funds, endowments, and ultra wealthy family offices had access to incredible, high yield assets like private real estate deals, energy projects, and early stage crypto.

Meanwhile, everyday retail investors were left fighting for scraps in overvalued stock markets or settling for savings accounts that didn't even beat inflation.

Stratmont was founded to level the playing field. By pooling capital and leveraging our team's deep industry connections, we unlock those exact same institutional grade opportunities for our clients. Today, we're proud to manage over $2.4 billion for people in dozens of countries, helping them secure their financial futures.

Our Journey

  • 2018: Stratmont is founded in New York with a focus on commercial real estate.
  • 2019: We cross our first major milestone: $100M in Assets Under Management.
  • 2021: Expanded our portfolio to include institutional grade Digital Assets and Crypto.
  • 2023: Launched our automated Client Portal, giving investors 24/7 access to their funds.
  • 2025: Surpassed $2.4B AUM, actively operating across 150+ global markets.

Our Process

How We Manage Your Money

Phase 1

Research & Analysis

Before we invest a single dollar, our analysts tear into the data. We look at global macro trends, geopolitical risks, and asset fundamentals. We reject 95% of the deals we look at, keeping only the strongest opportunities.

Phase 2

Portfolio Construction

We don't put all our eggs in one basket. We carefully allocate capital across Real Estate, Oil & Gas, and Crypto to ensure the portfolio is balanced. This way, if one sector dips, the others can carry the weight.

Phase 3

Active Management

We don't just 'set it and forget it.' We constantly monitor the markets, rebalancing the portfolio to lock in profits and minimize risks. You sit back and watch the returns roll into your dashboard.
