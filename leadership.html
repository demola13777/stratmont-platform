Executive Team

Guiding the Vision

Jonathan R. Caldwell

Chief Executive Officer

Former Managing Director at Goldman Sachs with over 20 years in global asset management. Jonathan holds an MBA from Harvard Business School and founded Stratmont to bring elite investment strategies to the broader market.

Dr. Aaron Osei

Chief Investment Officer

Marcus DuPont

Nikhil Sharma
