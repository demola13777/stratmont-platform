Our Energy Thesis

Why We Bet Big on Energy

Despite the headlines, the world still runs largely on traditional energy, and demand isn't dropping anytime soon. We see an opportunity to generate serious value by funding the infrastructure and projects that keep the lights on globally.

  • We spread our investments across upstream (extraction), midstream (transport), and downstream (refining) to capture profit at every stage.
  • We're pragmatic. We blend traditional oil and gas investments with forward looking energy transition projects.
  • We don't just hope prices stay high. We actively use commodity hedging to lock in profits and protect against market dips.
  • By taking direct stakes in energy projects, we cut out middlemen and capture better yields.

Investment Segments

How We Generate Returns

Upstream Exploration

This is where it all starts. We take equity stakes in proven drilling programs and secure rights to royalty streams. It's high reward, and we mitigate the risk by backing seasoned operators with a track record of finding reserves.

Midstream Infrastructure

Think pipelines, storage facilities, and transport. This is the "toll road" of the energy sector. Regardless of whether oil prices are up or down, operators have to pay to move their product, giving us stable, fee based revenue.

Energy Transition

We're looking to the future. We strategically invest in natural gas the vital bridge fuel as well as renewable adjacent technologies. It's about being positioned correctly as the world's energy mix gradually changes.

Global Operations

Where We Operate

Energy is a global game. To get the best returns and manage risk, we don't tie ourselves to just one country's regulations or geology.

  • North Sea (UK/Norway): Mature, highly regulated markets offering stability and proven reserves.
  • Gulf of Mexico (USA): A powerhouse of offshore production with massive existing infrastructure.
  • Middle East (UAE/KSA): Unmatched scale and some of the lowest extraction costs in the world.
  • West Africa (Nigeria/Ghana): High growth emerging markets with significant untapped potential.
  • Central Asia (Kazakhstan): Resource rich regions offering lucrative strategic partnerships.

How We Manage the Risks

Energy markets can be a wild ride. Here is how we make sure your investment stays safe even when oil prices are bouncing around.


Hedging the Price: We use financial contracts to lock in selling prices for our commodities ahead of time. If the market crashes tomorrow, our profit margin for the year is largely already secured.


Spreading it Out: A hurricane in the Gulf shouldn't ruin a portfolio. By having assets across the North Sea, Middle East, and the US, a localized problem rarely impacts our bottom line.


Doing it Right (ESG): We partner with operators who follow strict environmental and safety standards. It's not just about doing the right thing; it prevents costly fines, shutdowns, and PR disasters.
