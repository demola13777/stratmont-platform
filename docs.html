How the Platform Works

Stratmont operates as a collective investment platform. By pooling capital from thousands of clients, we gain the massive buying power required to access institutional grade assets like commercial real estate blocks, wholesale energy infrastructure, and private crypto deals.

When you select an investment plan, your funds are aggregated into our master portfolio. Our trading desk and algorithms then actively manage this capital across global markets to generate yield.

Note: We manage the complexity. As a client, your only tasks are selecting a plan that fits your goals and deciding whether to compound your returns or withdraw them at the end of a cycle.

Account Creation & Verification

Opening an account takes less than three minutes. However, to comply with global Anti Money Laundering (AML) laws, full account verification is required before making significant withdrawals.

The Verification Process

  • Submit a government issued ID (Passport or Driver's License).
  • Provide a recent proof of address (Utility bill or bank statement).
  • Enable Two Factor Authentication (2FA) via Google Authenticator or SMS.

Verification is typically completed within 24 hours.

Deposits & Funding

We support multiple funding methods to accommodate our global client base. Once deposited, your funds will appear in your 'Available Balance' immediately, allowing you to select an investment plan right away.

  • Cryptocurrency: We accept Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH), Solana (SOL), and Dogecoin (DOGE). This is the fastest and most preferred method.
  • Bank Wire Transfer: Available for Institutional clients making deposits over $50,000.

Withdrawals & Lock Periods

To generate high yields, our capital must be actively deployed in the market. Because of this, all standard investment plans operate on a strict 3-Month Cycle (90 Days).

The 90 Day Rule

Once you commit funds to a plan, your capital is "locked" for exactly 90 days. During this time, your balance will grow daily, but you cannot withdraw the initial principal or the accrued interest.

At the end of the 90 days, your cycle completes. You then have two choices:

  1. Withdraw: Request a payout of your initial capital plus all generated profits.
  2. Reinvest: Roll your new, larger balance into a new 90 day cycle to leverage the power of compound interest.
Early Withdrawals: Withdrawing funds before the 90 day cycle completes is generally not permitted, as it disrupts our market positions. Exceptions are only made in extreme emergencies and incur a severe penalty fee.

Fees & Charges

We believe in performance based alignment. We don't charge you for making deposits or holding an account.

  • Deposit Fee: 0%
  • Account Maintenance Fee: 0%
  • Withdrawal Fee: 1.5% network coverage fee (for crypto payouts).

Investment Strategies

We diversify across three major pillars to protect against sector specific downturns:

1. Real Estate

Focusing on commercial properties in high demand global cities. Provides steady rental yield and acts as a strong hedge against inflation.

2. Oil & Gas

Taking positions in upstream extraction and midstream pipeline infrastructure. We use hedging contracts to lock in profit margins regardless of daily price swings.

3. Digital Assets

Algorithmic trading of Bitcoin and Ethereum, alongside DeFi yield farming. This is our highest growth sector, managed with strict stop loss protocols.

Security Measures

We treat security as an existential requirement, not a feature. Your assets are protected by multiple layers of defense.

  • Cold Storage: The vast majority of our digital assets are held offline in air gapped, multi signature wallets.
  • Encryption: All user data and platform communications are secured using AES 256 military grade encryption.
  • Audits: Our smart contracts and trading algorithms are regularly audited by independent cybersecurity firms.
